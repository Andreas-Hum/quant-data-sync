Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Quant Data Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload and download the owner's own files to and from the owner's own Google Drive (through rclone).

  • Data accessed: only the files the tool itself creates in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Use: the files are copied between the owner's computer and the owner's Drive. Nothing else is done with them.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or shown to anyone.
  • Storage: the access token stays on the owner's computer. No server of this tool stores any data.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Andreas-Hum/quant-data-sync.
